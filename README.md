## Real-Time Detection of Spatial Disorientation in Mild Cognitive Impairment and Dementia

I wrote this review for *Research Areas in Computer Science*, a master’s course at the University of Rostock, under Prof. Thomas Kirste in 2026. It compares how researchers use wearable sensors and gait data to detect spatial disorientation, label behaviour from video, and evaluate models across participants.
